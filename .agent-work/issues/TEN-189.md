# TEN-189 — [P1] The pool is capped at two while the analytics page fires ten reads at once
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-189/p1-the-pool-is-capped-at-two-while-the-analytics-page-fires-ten-reads

**What breaks.** Pool `max: 2` (`lib/server/db.ts:30`). `app/(dashboard)/analytics/page.tsx:59` awaits the sink read, then `:62-73` runs `Promise.all` over ten loaders, each holding a client for three round trips. Ten reads serialise into five waves. With `statement_timeout: 10_000` the worst case is about 50 seconds before anything renders, and the checkout queue has no deadline of its own.

**Evidence.** `lib/server/db.ts:30`, `app/(dashboard)/analytics/page.tsx:59-73`. `/people` has the same shape at `app/(dashboard)/people/page.tsx:60-71`.

**Blast radius.** The two heaviest pages, exactly when the store is slow.

**How to know it is fixed.** The cap is above the widest fan-out, or the analytics reads are batched into fewer statements, plus a per page deadline. Count concurrent checkouts during one `/analytics` render.
