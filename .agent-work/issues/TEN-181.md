# TEN-181 — [P0] The complete score of record gate clears on a single track manifest
status: In Review
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-181/p0-the-complete-score-of-record-gate-clears-on-a-single-track-manifest

**What breaks.** The truth panel answers "A score of record measures what the spec says it measures" YES, and the route gate "One complete score of record" reads PASSED, from one score row covering one track. The same page declares T3 (160 points) unvalidated and T4 issuing no points.

**Evidence (live store plus the repo's own pure functions, run 2026-09-07).**

* `scores` holds exactly 1 live row: `track_id = t2`, `model_manifest.trackPoints = 80`, 4 components, every one `status = measured`, `partial = false`.
* `readScores()` returns `liveScores 1, completeScores 1, partialScores 0, latest total 80, measured 80, complete true`.
* `buildTruthChecks()` renders `YES | A score of record measures what the spec says it measures | points in the most recently scored sitting=80, of them measured=80`.
* `buildGtmRoute()` renders `passed | One complete score of record`. `lib/gtm.ts:270` reads the truth verdict by claim, so the two cannot disagree and both are wrong together.
* `lib/manifest.ts` computes coverage from the manifest's own `trackPoints`, which is per track. Nothing compares it to the instrument and nothing checks which tracks the sitting was dealt. `attempt_decks` for that attempt names t2 only.
* The premise in the `lib/manifest.ts` header is stale: it says the pipeline writes `partial: true` on every score it can produce. The stored row says `partial: false`.

**Blast radius.** The flagship check on `/truth`, the gate row on `/money`, and the "Gates cleared on the route" tile on the overview. A release gate that clears itself is worse than no gate.

**How to know it is fixed.** Coverage is computed against the instrument the sitting claims, not against one track's own denominator. A fixture with a single complete t2 manifest renders partial or unknown and does not clear the gate.
