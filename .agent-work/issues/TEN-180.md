# TEN-180 — [P0] Development identities are counted as registered users and their sittings as sittings
status: In Review
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-180/p0-development-identities-are-counted-as-registered-users-and-their

**What breaks.** Every headline figure counts `dev:` asserted development identities as real people. No query anywhere excludes them.

**Evidence (live store, read only, 2026-09-07).**

* `participants` holds 8 rows: 4 with an `auth_ref` under the `dev:` prefix, 4 under `clerk:`.
* `attempts`: 10 rows, 9 of them belonging to `dev:` participants. Real participant sittings: 1.
* Finalized attempts: 6, of which 5 are `dev:`. Real finalized sittings: 1.
* Credentials on `dev:` attempts: 3 of 4. Share links on `dev:` attempts: 2 of 2. Practice sessions: 1 of 7 is `dev:`.
* `lib/kpi.ts` `KPI_COUNTS_SQL` counts `participants WHERE auth_ref IS NOT NULL` as "Registered users" and `count(*) FROM attempts` as "Sittings started". Neither filters the prefix.
* The repo knows the distinction already: `features/people/model.ts:341` `providerOf()` splits `clerk:` from asserted, and the public contract has `isDevUserId` (`packages/contract/src/identity.ts:52`). The per-row table says "no Clerk account, asserted by the service". The aggregates ignore all of it.
* Downstream: `lib/truth.ts` renders "People have completed a sitting: YES" on 6 completions of which 5 are development. `lib/tiles.ts` renders the same counts as overview tiles. The analytics funnel, track progress, share loop and day series all inherit it.

**Blast radius.** Every counted figure except the front-of-funnel browser counts and practice. This is the number a founder would quote in a funding conversation, and it is wrong in the flattering direction.

**How to know it is fixed.** One predicate in one place, applied to every participant-derived query: exclude asserted identities from the counted figures, or count them and render "of them development" beside each. A test that seeds one `dev:` and one `clerk:` participant asserts the headline count is one, not two.
