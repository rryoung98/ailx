# TEN-130 — Hosted mode has no path from a completed run to a share or a credential
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-130/hosted-mode-has-no-path-from-a-completed-run-to-a-share-or-a

**Observed 2026-09-04, finalized live run** `856b850c-51ac-4a1a-82a5-24deb8df66ae`**.**

`POST https://ailx-backend-932932410694.us-central1.run.app/v1/attempts/:id/share` -> **400** `nothing to share yet — finish and score every track first`.

That gate cannot be satisfied in the hosted build:

* T1 and T4 are JUDGED tracks. The service refuses to score them by design: `POST /v1/attempts/:id/score {"trackId":"t1"}` -> **400** `attempt was dealt no t1 deck` (same for t4). They are scored only in the browser ("demo estimate" / "showcase, not scored").
* The share gate requires every track scored ON THE SERVICE.

So no candidate can publish, appear on the wall or gallery, produce a share card, or issue a credential. /gallery confirms it: `GET /v1/gallery` -> 200 with `total: 0`.

**Fix direction:** decide what the share gate means for locally scored judged tracks, and make the two halves agree.
