# TEN-196 — [P2] The KPI return cohorts and the people page use two different definitions of active
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-196/p2-the-kpi-return-cohorts-and-the-people-page-use-two-different

**What breaks.** The same word means two things on two pages, and neither page says the other exists.

**Evidence.** `lib/kpi.ts` `KPI_COUNTS_SQL` builds its `activity` CTE from `attempts.started_at` and `practice_sessions.started_at` only. `features/people/sql.ts` `ACTIVE_DAYS_SQL` and `ACTIVE_WINDOWS_SQL` build theirs from four events: sitting started, sitting finalized, practice started, practice completed, and `features/people/model.ts` prints that definition on screen as the page's chosen one. A person who finalized on the day after they started counts as returning on `/people` and does not on `/kpi`.

**Blast radius.** D1 and D7 return, which are two of the six figures a funder asks for. Both are withheld today for a small cohort, so the defect is latent and lands the first time the cohort reaches twenty.

**How to know it is fixed.** One definition of an active day, shared by both queries, printed once. A fixture where a participant only finalizes on day one gives the same answer on both pages.
