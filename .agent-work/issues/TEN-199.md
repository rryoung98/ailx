# TEN-199 — [P2] The share step is labelled seen by someone else and counts views the owner may have made
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-199/p2-the-share-step-is-labelled-seen-by-someone-else-and-counts-views

**What breaks.** The funnel step "Was seen by someone else" counts rows in `share_views`, and nothing in that table can tell the owner's own view from a stranger's.

**Evidence.** `features/analytics/model.ts` `buildFunnel()`, the last step. `share_views` holds `id, share_id, viewed_on` and nothing else: no visitor id, no referrer, by design. Store today: 7 rows across 2 links, and 2 of 2 shared attempts belong to development identities.

**Blast radius.** The bottom of the sitting funnel, which is the step that would be read as proof of a loop. The step's own definition text is accurate; the label is the claim a reader takes away.

**How to know it is fixed.** The label says what the row proves, for example "the share card was served", and the definition keeps its caveat.
