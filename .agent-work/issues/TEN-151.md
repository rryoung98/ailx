# TEN-151 — Two copy lines tell a signed-in candidate they have no account: /exam's access pill and the daily result screen
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-151/two-copy-lines-tell-a-signed-in-candidate-they-have-no-account-exams

Small, but it is what a signed-in candidate reads tonight (staging, 2026-09-05).

* /exam header pill: "sign in to sit a scored run" — `apps/web/lib/mode.ts` `examAccessCopy()`
  returns this whenever Clerk is enabled, regardless of whether the reader is signed in. A
  signed-in candidate is told to do the thing they just did.
* /daily result screen: "Your streak lives on this device only: clear your browser data and it
  is gone. There is no account to lose it to." — said to a candidate who has an account, on a
  deployment that has accounts.

Both are unconditional strings on identity-aware pages. Fixing them needs the identity seam from
<issue id="e1efbe6b-1ebe-4853-a3e8-316671de1fba" href="https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-148/progress-refuses-a-signed-in-candidate-identity-required-reads-fire">TEN-148</issue>, so they are worth doing in the same pass.
