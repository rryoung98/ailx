# TEN-144 — Funnel: visit_started is not emitted on six top-level routes, so D1/D7 undercounts return
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-144/funnel-visit-started-is-not-emitted-on-six-top-level-routes-so-d1d7

**Found by driving deployed staging ([https://ailx-staging.vercel.app](<https://ailx-staging.vercel.app>)) on 2026-09-05, first night** `/v1/events` **was live.**

`visit_started` is emitted by `session()` inside `apps/web/lib/data/funnel.ts`, on the first event a page asks the emitter for. A route that emits no other step therefore emits nothing at all — no client id is minted, no session is opened, no row is posted.

Verified in the browser on the deployed site. Six routes are dark:
`/progress`, `/report`, `/gallery`, `/world`, `/validate`, `/methodology`.
After loading each one and waiting past the flush window, `localStorage["ailx.funnel.client.v1"]` was `null` and `funnel_events` gained zero rows.

**Why it matters.** docs/KPI.md step 1 says `visit_started` means "a browser opened Foray", and the admin analytics page words it "a browser opened Foray at all". Neither is what the code counts. Worse, `/progress` is the destination the practice and daily result screens link to ("See your progress"), so it is the most likely *return* surface — and D1/D7 is derived from client ids that emitted something. A player who practises on day 0 and comes back on day 1 to look at their streak emits nothing, and is counted as not returned. D1 is biased low at exactly the place the product expects return.

**Measured in this run.** 12 browser sessions opened the site. `funnel_events` holds 10 `visit_started`. The two missing are the sessions that only read non-instrumented pages (one of them six page loads).

**Fix (pushed, not merged):** branch `w/funnel-visit` on rryoung98/ailx, commit `633c103`.

* `funnel().visit()` — a new entry point that only opens the session.
* `apps/web/components/FunnelVisit.tsx`, mounted in `app/layout.tsx` outside `AuthShell` so it does not depend on Clerk being configured.
* Four tests in `apps/web/test/funnelSurfaces.test.tsx`: one visit and nothing else, one visit across remounts and a reload, no interference with a real step, and silence with no backend.
* `pnpm test` green (3138 passed, 38 skipped). `pnpm lint` reports no new diagnostics.
