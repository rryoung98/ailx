# TEN-195 — [P2] Analytics and KPI day buckets are cast without a timezone while people buckets are cast with one
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-195/p2-analytics-and-kpi-day-buckets-are-cast-without-a-timezone-while

**What breaks.** Two pages can put the same sitting on different days, and the boundary moves with a connection setting.

**Evidence.** `features/analytics/sql.ts` uses `started_at::date` and `finalized_at::date` (`ATTEMPTS_PER_DAY_SQL`, `PRACTICE_PER_DAY_SQL`); `lib/kpi.ts` `KPI_COUNTS_SQL` does the same and compares against `current_date`. `features/people/sql.ts` pins `AT TIME ZONE 'UTC'` on every cast and explains at length why: "a day boundary that moves with a connection setting is not a measurement". The live store reports `TimeZone = GMT`, so the two agree today by accident of configuration, not by construction.

**Blast radius.** The day series on `/analytics`, the D1 and D7 cohorts on `/kpi`, and any comparison a reader makes between those and `/people`.

**How to know it is fixed.** Every cast names the zone. Run one fixture under `SET TIME ZONE 'Pacific/Auckland'` and assert the buckets do not move.
