# TEN-200 — [P2] An out of range page number renders zero matching people
status: Backlog
url: https://linear.app/tenken/issue/TEN-200/p2-an-out-of-range-page-number-renders-zero-matching-people

**What breaks.** `features/people/load.ts` takes the total from `rows[0]?.total ?? 0`, so a page past the last one has no rows and reports a total of zero. `features/people/PeoplePanel.tsx:227` then renders "0 matching people" for a store that holds eight, and it renders that zero as a digit through `formatCount`, bypassing the rule that a counted zero must be words.

**Evidence.** `features/people/load.ts` `loadPeoplePage` (`const total = rows[0]?.total ?? 0`), `features/people/model.ts` `parsePeopleQuery` (page is any positive integer, never clamped), `features/people/PeoplePanel.tsx:227`.

**Blast radius.** Anyone who edits the URL or follows a stale pager link. A wrong figure with no standing next to it, on the page that carries identities.

**How to know it is fixed.** The page is clamped to the page count, or the total comes from its own count and an empty page says so in words. `?page=99` renders the real total.
