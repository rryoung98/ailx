## Generated for the practice corpus

Five images were made for this corpus rather than found, because found
generations are scarce and are rarely culturally specific enough to be
culturally WRONG — which left the sociocultural family nearly empty. They are
prompted through OpenRouter and spread across BOTH provider families and
across model generations on purpose: a corpus from one generator teaches that
generator's fingerprint instead of the artefact.

We may republish them. OpenAI assigns output rights to the customer ("We
hereby assign to you all our right, title, and interest, if any, in and to
Output", Terms of Use, *Ownership of content*); Google does not claim them
("Google won't claim ownership over that content", Gemini API Terms, *Use of
Generated Content*); OpenRouter claims nothing and passes the model terms
through (ToS §6.1). Checked 2026-08-30. Both providers require that the AI
origin is never denied — this corpus exists to state it. Output carries
invisible SynthID (and, from OpenAI, C2PA) provenance signals; re-encoding may
drop C2PA, and nothing here is ever presented as a human photograph. We
release our own generated images as CC0.

The full prompt, the model, the date, the OpenRouter generation id, the cost
and the vetting verdict for every attempt — including the ones REJECTED after
a person looked at them — are in
`instruments/practice/2026.1/generated.json`.

# Player-type character credits

The sixteen player-type characters (`apps/web/public/characters/*.jpg`) were
generated for AILX, one per type code, and are released as CC0. They are the
face of the share card (`docs/UX-DIRECTION.md`: "the player-type card is our
owl"); the name, the tagline and the four-letter code beside them are written
by hand and live in `packages/report/src/playerType.ts`.

Unlike the practice corpus, the whole cast is drawn by ONE model from ONE
shared style paragraph. That is deliberate: the practice corpus spreads across
generators so a candidate cannot learn one fingerprint, whereas sixteen
characters that do not share a line weight and a palette are not a cast.

We may republish them on the same basis as the practice generations: Google
does not claim ownership of output ("Google won't claim ownership over that
content", Gemini API Terms, *Use of Generated Content*) and OpenRouter passes
the model terms through (ToS §6.1). Checked 2026-08-30. Output carries
invisible SynthID provenance, and the AI origin is stated here rather than
denied.

Every attempt — including the three REJECTED after a person looked at them —
carries its full prompt, model, date, generation id, cost and vetting reason
in `instruments/characters/2026.1/generated.json`. The shipped manifest is
